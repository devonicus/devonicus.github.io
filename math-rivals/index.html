Math Rivals

1v1 mental math duels. 90 seconds, the same board, and another player's run to beat.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

How it plays

Drag across touching tiles whose numbers add up to the target. Longer paths and gold tiles score more, and quick clears build a combo. Every duel is the same board for both players: you race another player's recorded run, shown live as a progress bar, so there's no waiting for an opponent to be online.

The Daily Board is the same for everyone each day, with one scored try, and every duel earns points in a weekly league. Free to play, with an optional Rivals Club subscription that never buys points or wins.

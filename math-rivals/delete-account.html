Delete your Math Rivals account

Math Rivals: 1v1 Math Duel by Devonicus

Delete in the game (fastest)

Open Math Rivals › Settings (⚙) › Delete account › Delete permanently. This deletes straight away:

  • your profile (generated name, colour, age group, level, coins, streak, weekly points)
  • your recorded runs, scores, duel history, head-to-head records and inbox
  • your notification token and your sign-in account (including any Apple or Google link)

Delete without the app

Email privacy@devonicus.com with the subject “Delete my Math Rivals account” and your in-game name (shown in Settings and on your profile). If you linked Apple or Google, send it from that email address. We delete the same data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What isn't deleted

Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for legal and accounting reasons. A Rivals Club subscription isn't cancelled by deleting your account: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

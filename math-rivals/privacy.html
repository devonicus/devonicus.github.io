Privacy Policy

Math Rivals: 1v1 Math Duel · Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android and iOS game Math Rivals (“the game”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Devonicus, based in Australia (“we”, “us”). Questions: privacy@devonicus.com.

The short version

  • You play as a guest. We create a random account ID for you; we never ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Your display name is generated by the game (for example “SwiftOtter42”). There is no chat and no free-text name.
  • We store your scores and recorded runs so other players can race them, plus your level, streak and head-to-head records.
  • We ask for an age group (not your birthday) so we can keep under-13s out of duels and set up ads correctly.
  • Ads are optional reward videos only, shown by Google AdMob. We don't ask to track you across other companies' apps.
  • The app sends us anonymous play statistics (for example how many duels were played in a visit) through TelemetryDeck. They contain no name, email, advertising ID or location.
  • The optional Rivals Club subscription goes through Google Play or the App Store, checked by RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time from Settings › Delete account, or at devonicus.com/math-rivals/delete-account.html.

Services that process information for us

Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging)

Stores your account ID, profile, scores and runs, checks runs, and sends notifications. Servers are in the United States. See the Firebase privacy information.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The game offers optional reward videos (for example “Double coins”). To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software collects device identifiers, your IP address (which gives an approximate location such as your country or city), ad interactions and diagnostics. The game does not ask iPhone users for permission to track, so ads on iPhone are not personalised using your advertising identifier. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before personalised ads, and you can change your choice later in Settings › Privacy choices. Players under 16 are tagged as minors so they don't get personalised ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Anonymous play statistics: TelemetryDeck

To understand how the game is played and what to improve, the app sends anonymous statistics to us through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany. They are sent when you open the app and when you leave it, plus a few one-off moments (finishing the practice board, your first duel, a purchase), and contain:

  • game numbers, such as your level, streak, how many duels you played and won in a visit, and whether you have the Rivals Club or No ads
  • your device platform (Android or iOS)
  • a random identifier that the app creates when first opened, separate from your account ID. TelemetryDeck hashes it again before storing it, so it can't be traced back to your device, your name or your email

The statistics don't include your name, email address, advertising ID or location, are not used for advertising or tracking, and are not shared with anyone else. TelemetryDeck doesn't store IP addresses. We only see combined numbers.

Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat (purchases)

Rivals Club payments are handled by Google Play or Apple. RevenueCat confirms purchases and receives your random account ID, your purchase history in this game (product, date, price, currency) and basic technical information (device type, operating system, app version, IP address for store country). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

What other players see

Your generated name, colour, level, scores, recorded runs (the moves you made on a board) and head-to-head results with them. Nothing else.

Children

Math Rivals is for players aged 13 and over. If a player selects “under 13”, duels, ads and all online features are switched off and only practice boards are available, played on the device with nothing sent to us. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

  • Profile, streak and head-to-head records: until you delete your account. Guest accounts that are never used again may be removed after 12 months of inactivity.
  • Recorded runs: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Duel tickets: 1 day. Rival notifications in your inbox: 14 days.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google, Apple and RevenueCat as their policies and the law require.

Deleting your account

In the game: Settings › Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, runs, scores, streak, rival history and your sign-in account from our servers. Without the app, use the request form at delete-account.html. A Rivals Club subscription must be cancelled in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, the UK and EU GDPR and US state privacy laws) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your information. Email privacy@devonicus.com with your in-game name (Settings shows it) and we will respond within 30 days. In Australia you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner; in the UK/EEA, to your data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Some US state laws treat sharing information with advertising partners as a “sale” or “sharing”; you can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings and in Settings › Privacy choices where shown.

International transfers and security

Information is processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate, with the safeguards the law requires. All information is encrypted in transit, and the database can only be reached through our checked server functions.

Changes

If the game changes how it handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

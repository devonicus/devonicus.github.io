Holiday Express

1 December – 5 January. Snow, fairy lights and eight festive pictures on the daily line: a snowman, gingerbread, a stocking, a bauble and more. Finish five festive dailies to earn the Holiday Express sticker. The decorations can be switched off in the pause menu.

How it plays

A little wooden train runs round a loop of track, and each wagon carries blocks of one colour. Send the wagons round the picture to clear it, one colour at a time. Park a wagon in a siding when its colour isn't needed yet, but there are only so many sidings, so plan ahead.

Levels that get trickier as you go, a new daily picture every day, and an album of stickers to collect. Free to play, with optional ads and a one-off Remove ads purchase.

Testers

Thanks for helping test our games before they launch. Pick your phone below.

Android

  1. Open Join the test while signed in to the Google account we added to the tester list, and tap Become a tester.
  2. Then tap Google Play to install the game. If it isn't there yet, wait a few minutes and try again.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Install Apple's free TestFlight app.
  2. Open the game's TestFlight link on the same device and tap Accept, then Install. If it says the beta isn't accepting testers yet, Apple is still checking the latest build: try again in a day.

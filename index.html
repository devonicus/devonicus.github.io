Biscuit's Bookshop Mysteries

Fill the shelves. Find the clues. Solve the case.

Biscuit is the resident cat of the Salt & Page, a little bookshop in the seaside village of Little Wending. When the shop's prized antique atlas vanishes the night before it reopens, Biscuit sets out to find out who took it.

Drag books onto the shelf to clear rows and columns. Clear a line through a clue to add it to Biscuit's notebook, then decide who did it.

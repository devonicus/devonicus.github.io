Shelf Life

A corner-shop sorting puzzle. Sort the stock, sell the shelves and survive the week.

Download on the App Store Google Play (beta)

Free, with optional ads and a one-off Remove ads purchase. iPhone and iPad. Android is in a closed beta: email contact@devonicus.com to join.

How it plays

Run a little corner shop one board at a time. Tap a shelf to lift its front stock and tap another to put it down; fill a shelf with four of a kind and it sells. Sell in quick succession for combos worth up to five times the coins.

Each shop day has a Morning, an Afternoon and a Closing Rush with a twist: Fog, Rush Hour or a Big Delivery. Between days, spend your takings at the supplier's van on relics that change the rules for the rest of the run. Every board is generated fresh and always solvable, and the Daily Shift gives everyone the same three boards each day.

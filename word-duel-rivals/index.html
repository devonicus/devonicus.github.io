Word Duel Rivals

Three racks. Ninety seconds. One rival. A daily word puzzle and 1v1 word duels.

Free on the App Store and Google Play, with an optional Rivals Club subscription.

Advent and Fresh Start

1 – 25 December: the Daily Board becomes an Advent calendar, and every board ends on a festive Final Rack. Play any 20 of the 25 days for the Advent badge.

26 December – 8 January: the Fresh Start fortnight carries festive racks into the new year. Play any 7 of the 14 days for the Fresh Start badge, then share your year in words.

How it plays

You and your rival get the same seven letter tiles, three racks in a row, 30 seconds each. Spell as many words as you can: longer words score more, quick-fire words build a bonus, and every rack hides a seven-letter crown word. The last rack scores double, so the lead can change in the final seconds.

Your rival is another player's recorded run on exactly the same racks, so a duel starts the moment you tap. Every day there's one Daily Board for everyone, with one scored try, and every duel earns points for this week's board. After each round you see every rack's crown word and the best words you missed. More than 50,000 words, with Australian, British and American spellings all counted.

Free to play, with an optional Rivals Club subscription that never buys points or wins.

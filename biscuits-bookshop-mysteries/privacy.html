Privacy Policy

Biscuit's Bookshop Mysteries · Last updated 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Biscuit's Bookshop Mysteries (“the game”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Devonicus, based in Australia (“we”, “us”). If you have any questions, email privacy@devonicus.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address or contact details.
  • Your progress, coins and clues are saved only on your device. They aren't sent to us.
  • The game shows ads using Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • The game sends us anonymous play statistics, such as how far you are in the story and how often you play the daily puzzle, through TelemetryDeck. They contain no name, email, advertising ID or location.
  • In-app purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. We use RevenueCat to check what you've bought. We never see your card details.
  • If you are in the UK or EEA, you are asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device, such as levels completed, coins, clues found, sound settings, how many reward videos you have watched today, and whether you have removed ads or joined the Detective's Club. The game's fonts and artwork are built in. New weekly cases (their story, pictures and recorded voices) are downloaded from GitHub, where we publish them, and kept on your device so you can play them offline; downloading them sends nothing about you apart from what any web request includes, such as your IP address. This stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. It is not sent to us, apart from the anonymous play statistics described below.

Information collected by services we use

Advertising: Google AdMob

The game shows optional reward videos and occasional ads between levels using Google AdMob, provided by Google. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the game automatically collects:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (for example, your country or city, not your precise location)
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps
  • diagnostic information about how the app and ads perform

This information is sent to and handled by Google. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads; on iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking). On iPhone, the game asks for permission before using your advertising identifier. In the UK and EEA, the game shows Google's consent form before personalised ads, and you can change your choice later from Ad privacy choices in the game's shop. Players who buy Remove ads or join the Detective's Club don't see ads between levels.

Anonymous play statistics: TelemetryDeck

To understand how the game is played and when to add new cases, the game sends anonymous statistics to us through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany. They are sent when you open the game, when you leave it, when you solve a case for the first time and when you make a purchase, and contain:

  • game numbers, such as how many cases you have solved, the level you are on, your coins, your daily puzzle streak, and how many levels, hints and daily puzzles you played in that visit
  • your device platform (Android or iOS)
  • a random identifier that the game creates when first opened. TelemetryDeck hashes it again before storing it, so it can't be traced back to your device, your name or your email

The statistics don't include your name, email address, advertising ID or location, are not used for advertising or tracking, and are not shared with anyone else. TelemetryDeck doesn't store IP addresses. We only see combined numbers, such as how many players have solved every case.

Purchases: Google Play, the App Store and RevenueCat

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple's App Store. They handle your payment details, and we never receive your card or bank information. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and keep track of what you own (for example, so that Remove ads stays active and can be restored). RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier that the game creates, which is not linked to your name or email
  • your purchase history in this game: which products you bought, when, and their price and currency

RevenueCat also receives basic technical information needed to process purchases, such as your device type, operating system and app version, and your IP address (used to determine your store country).

See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy, the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

All information sent by the game is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal information for money. Some US state laws treat sharing information with advertising partners as a “sale” or “sharing”. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings, as described above.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and you would like information about them removed, contact us at the address below.

How long information is kept

Information on your device is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Google, Apple and RevenueCat keep information according to their own privacy policies. Anonymous play statistics are kept in TelemetryDeck while they are useful for improving the game. RevenueCat keeps purchase records so purchases can be restored and for legal and accounting reasons.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Australian Privacy Act 1988, the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation, and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it is used. Because the game has no accounts, we usually can't identify you from the information above. If you contact us, we will help where we can and pass requests to Google, Apple or RevenueCat as needed. You can also delete all locally saved data by uninstalling the game.

If you are in Australia and are not satisfied with our response to a privacy complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

International transfers

Google, Apple and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. TelemetryDeck processes the anonymous play statistics in the European Union. They use safeguards required by law for these transfers.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and the date at the top of this page.

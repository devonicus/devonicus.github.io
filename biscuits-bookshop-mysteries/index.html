Biscuit's Bookshop Mysteries

Fill the shelves. Find the clues. Solve the case.

Biscuit is the resident cat of the Salt & Page, a little bookshop in the seaside village of Little Wending. Nothing gets past Biscuit, which is lucky, because Little Wending has rather a lot of mysteries.

Drag books onto the shelf to clear rows and columns. Clear a line through a magnifying glass to add a clue to Biscuit's notebook. Then decide who did it.

Three cases to solve

Case 1: The Vanishing Atlas

The night before the bookshop reopens, its prized antique sea atlas disappears. Who took it, and why?

Case 2: The Lighthouse Letters

A bundle of letters from 1893 sets the whole village hunting for treasure. Biscuit thinks the paper is too young.

Case 3: The Clockmaker's Key

Every night the church clock stops at 3:17. Someone is climbing the tower.

Case 4: The Regatta Cup is coming soon.

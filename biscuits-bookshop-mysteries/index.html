A bookshop cat with a mystery to solve

Biscuit is the resident cat of the Salt & Page, a little bookshop in the seaside village of Little Wending. Nothing gets past Biscuit, which is lucky, because Little Wending has rather a lot of mysteries.

How it plays

Drag books onto the 8×8 shelf. Fill a row or column to clear it, and clear a line through a magnifying glass to add a clue to Biscuit's notebook. Every level is a designed puzzle that's been proven solvable, not endless random blocks. When the evidence is in, you name the culprit yourself, and see if you were right first time.

Five cases to solve

Case 1: The Vanishing Atlas

The night before the bookshop reopens, its prized antique sea atlas disappears. Who took it, and why?

Case 2: The Lighthouse Letters

A bundle of letters from 1893 sets the whole village hunting for treasure. Biscuit thinks the paper is too young.

Case 3: The Clockmaker's Key

Every night the church clock stops at 3:17. Someone is climbing the tower.

Case 4: The Regatta Cup

Three days before the Little Wending Regatta, the silver cup vanishes from the harbour office. Nothing else is touched.

Case 5: The Forgotten Parcel

A crate lost in the post in 1951 turns up for the Salt & Page. Overnight, someone opens it and takes only a letter.

Case 6: The Silent Bell is coming soon.

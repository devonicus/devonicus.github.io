Privacy Policy

Plush Pirates · Last updated 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android and iOS app Plush Pirates (“the game”) collects. The game is a learning game for children, made by Devonicus, based in Australia (“we”, “us”). If you have any questions, email privacy@devonicus.com.

The short version

  • We don't collect any information. Nothing your child does in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no way to contact other players.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Progress, settings and drawings are saved only on the device.

What stays on the device

The game saves your child's progress (skills practised, levels, rewards earned), the settings chosen in the grown-ups area (text size, letter spacing, background colour, voice speed, sound) and how many minutes have been played each day, so the grown-ups area can show progress. This is stored only on the device. You can remove it at any time with Reset progress in the grown-ups area, or by uninstalling the game.

Voice

Most of the game's voice is recorded and built into the app. Some maths questions are read aloud by the device's own text-to-speech voice. That happens on the device; the game does not send the words anywhere.

Purchases

If the game offers a one-off purchase to unlock more islands, it is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store, behind a check for grown-ups. We never see payment details. The game only asks the store whether the unlock has been bought.

Children

The game is designed for children. Because it collects no personal information, it does not ask for parental consent to collect data. It meets the Google Play Families policy and the Apple Kids category rules: no advertising, no third-party analytics, and links or purchases only behind a check for grown-ups.

Permissions

The game does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos. It does not use the advertising ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
